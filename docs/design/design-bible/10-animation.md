# Motion & Animation

## Philosophy

Animation exists to communicate.

Not entertain.

Players should feel the interface.

Not notice the animation.

---

# Motion Goals

Guide attention

Provide feedback

Reduce uncertainty

Increase delight

---

# Principles

Fast

Smooth

Purposeful

Consistent

Interruptible

---

# Use Motion For

Navigation

State Change

Loading

Success

Failure

Selection

Hover

Press

Completion

---

# Never Animate

Large blocks of text

Entire layouts

Constant looping backgrounds

Everything at once

---

# Gameplay

Correct Answer

↓

Small positive feedback

Wrong Answer

↓

Gentle correction

Victory

↓

Celebration

Failure

↓

Encouragement

---

# Transition

Transitions should preserve context.

Avoid hard cuts whenever possible.

---

# Loading

Skeleton preferred.

Spinner only when unavoidable.

---

# Micro Interaction

Buttons

↓

Slight press feedback

Cards

↓

Small elevation

Selection

↓

Soft highlight

Achievement

↓

Celebration

---

# Achievement Animation

Short.

Rewarding.

Never longer than necessary.

---

# Confetti

Use only for

Major achievements.

Never after every game.

---

# Sound

Sound and animation should reinforce each other.

Never compete.

---

# Accessibility

Support

Reduced Motion.

Animations should respect user preferences.

---

# Overall Feeling

Calm.

Polished.

Professional.

Rewarding.

Never overwhelming.
