# Content Guidelines

## Philosophy

Words are part of the user experience.

The interface should sound like a supportive coach, not a teacher or examiner.

Every message should help players continue playing.

---

## Tone of Voice

The tone should always be:

- Friendly
- Encouraging
- Clear
- Short
- Positive
- Confident

Avoid sounding:

- Robotic
- Academic
- Corporate
- Aggressive
- Judgmental

---

## Writing Principles

Write short sentences.

Prefer verbs over nouns.

Avoid technical language.

Avoid unnecessary punctuation.

One idea per sentence.

---

## Button Labels

Use action verbs.

Examples

Play

Continue

Retry

Save

Join

Share

View Profile

Settings

Back

Avoid

Click Here

Submit

Execute

Confirm Action

---

## Titles

Keep titles concise.

Examples

Daily Challenge

Your Progress

Leaderboard

Game History

Achievements

Avoid long descriptive titles.

---

## Empty States

Every empty state should contain:

- Friendly title
- Short explanation
- Suggested next action

Example

Title

No games played yet

Description

Start your first challenge and begin improving your memory.

Action

Play Now

---

## Error Messages

Always explain:

- What happened
- What the player can do next

Example

Connection lost

Please check your internet connection and try again.

Action

Retry

Avoid technical error codes.

---

## Success Messages

Celebrate progress.

Examples

Great job!

New personal best!

Nice memory!

Challenge completed!

Well done!

Avoid exaggerated praise.

---

## Failure Messages

Encourage retry.

Examples

Almost!

Try Again

You're getting better.

One more round?

Avoid

Game Over

Mission Failed

Incorrect

Failure

---

## Achievement Text

Keep achievement names memorable.

Examples

Quick Learner

Sharp Eyes

Memory Master

Pattern Expert

Lightning Recall

---

## Leaderboard

Show ranking respectfully.

Avoid language that shames lower-ranked players.

---

## Notifications

Keep notifications under two lines.

Examples

Your friend beat your record.

Daily Challenge is ready.

New achievement unlocked.

---

## Localization

Avoid culture-specific jokes.

Avoid slang.

Keep wording easy to translate.

---

## Accessibility

Avoid using emoji as the only meaning.

Always provide text.
