# Game UI Guidelines

## Philosophy

Gameplay is the product.

The interface disappears.

The game remains.

---

## Gameplay Priority

Gameplay should occupy approximately 80% of the screen.

HUD should remain lightweight.

Menus should stay hidden.

---

## Gameplay Layout

Top

↓

HUD

↓

Gameplay Area

↓

Input Area

↓

Optional Controls

---

## HUD

Contains only essential information.

Examples

Score

Level

Timer

Lives

Streak

Nothing else.

---

## Gameplay Area

Always receives visual priority.

No advertisements.

No notifications.

No floating widgets.

---

## Input Area

Easy to reach.

Thumb friendly.

Immediate feedback.

---

## Pause

Pause should freeze gameplay.

Never restart automatically.

---

## Retry

Retry should be instant.

Avoid confirmation dialogs.

Players retry frequently.

---

## Success Feedback

Immediate.

Positive.

Short.

Examples

Small animation

Soft sound

Score increase

Progress update

---

## Failure Feedback

Encouraging.

Never punishing.

Examples

Try Again

Almost!

One More Round?

Avoid

Game Over

Mission Failed

Incorrect

---

## Timer

Readable.

Consistent position.

Never jump around.

---

## Score

Smooth updates.

No flashing.

No unnecessary animation.

---

## Combo

Celebrate combos.

Do not interrupt gameplay.

---

## Streak

Reward consistency.

Display naturally.

Avoid pressure.

---

## Progress

Always show progress when useful.

Examples

Level 12 / 20

Sequence 8 / 15

Round 4 / 10

---

## Difficulty

Clearly visible.

Players should always know current difficulty.

---

## Session Length

Target

30 seconds

↓

3 minutes

Quick sessions encourage replay.

---

## Interruption

Never interrupt gameplay with

Rating dialogs

Promotions

Notifications

Friend requests

---

## Sound

Optional.

Always configurable.

---

## Haptic

Optional.

Never required.

---

## Accessibility

Support

Reduced Motion

High Contrast

Large Text

Color Blind Mode

---

## Daily Challenge

Should feel special.

Never mandatory.

---

## Ranking

Show after gameplay.

Never during gameplay.

---

## Achievement

Celebrate after the game.

Never interrupt the game.

---

## Multiplayer

Opponent information should never hide gameplay.

---

## Spectator

Gameplay remains primary.

Chat remains secondary.

---

## Replay

One tap.

Players should replay immediately.

---

## Future Games

Every future game should reuse

HUD

Timer

Score

Progress

Pause

Result

without redesigning the interface.
