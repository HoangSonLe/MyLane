# Design Principles

## Principle 01

Gameplay First

Everything exists to support gameplay.

Remove anything that distracts from the game.

---

## Principle 02

One Primary CTA

Every screen has exactly one primary action.

Users should never hesitate about what to do next.

---

## Principle 03

Progressive Disclosure

Do not show everything at once.

Reveal information only when needed.

Examples

Home

↓

Play

↓

Choose Game

↓

Difficulty

↓

Game

instead of

Everything on one screen.

---

## Principle 04

Recognition over Recall

Users should recognize actions instantly.

Do not rely on memory to use the interface.

---

## Principle 05

Large Touch Targets

Every important action should be easy to tap.

Design for thumbs.

---

## Principle 06

Minimal HUD

Gameplay screens contain only essential information.

Hide everything else.

---

## Principle 07

Whitespace

Whitespace is part of the design.

Do not fill empty space just because it exists.

---

## Principle 08

Hierarchy

Every screen must answer:

Where am I?

What should I do?

What happens next?

---

## Principle 09

Component Consistency

The same button always looks the same.

The same card always behaves the same.

Do not redesign components per screen.

---

## Principle 10

Accessibility

Readable.

Reachable.

Understandable.

Inclusive.

---

## Principle 11

Motion with Purpose

Animation communicates.

Animation should never distract.

---

## Principle 12

Reward Frequently

Small wins.

Small celebrations.

Smooth feedback.

Avoid giant reward screens.

---

## Principle 13

Expand Naturally

Adding a new game should require almost no UI redesign.

Only new content.

Not new navigation.

---

## Principle 14

Less Interface

Whenever possible

remove

instead of

adding.
